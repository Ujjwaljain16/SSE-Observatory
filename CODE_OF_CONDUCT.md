# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

**Our pledge.** We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**What we expect.** Be kind and patient. Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Take responsibility for mistakes, learn from them, and focus on what is best for the community. Criticise code and ideas, not people.

**What we do not accept.** Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, trolling, publishing someone's private information, and anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Enforcement.** The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may restrict anyone who behaves in ways they judge harmful. To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository (Security, then Report a vulnerability, which also takes conduct reports) or contact the maintainer, [@Ujjwaljain16](https://github.com/Ujjwaljain16), through GitHub. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

This applies in all project spaces: the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any place where someone represents the project.
